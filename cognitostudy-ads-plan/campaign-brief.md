## Brand DNA Summary

CognitoStudy speaks with a directo, motivador, tecnológico-cercano voice — formal 7/10, bold 8/10, innovative 9/10 — addressing students 18-30 in Spain and LATAM who are exhausted by manual note-summarising and oposiciones-scale temarios. Visually it lives in a nocturno-digital aesthetic: deep `#0F0F1A` backgrounds, bright `#6C63FF` primary accents with `#00C9A7` and `#3B82F6` secondaries, generous negative space and high-contrast UI/illustration hybrids that feel product-led and digital-native. Every campaign asset must reinforce two ideas simultaneously: "your notes are unstructured chaos" and "AI turns them into a complete study system in segundos."

---

## Audit Context

No prior audit data is available — this is the first campaign for CognitoStudy. Concepts are therefore designed for cold-audience prospecting and broad-angle testing, with three distinctly different messaging hooks (transformation demo, niche high-intent segment, competitor challenger) so post-launch performance data can identify the strongest creative territory for scaling. For sharper targeting on round 2, run `/ads audit` after 14 days of spend.

## Campaign Concepts

### Concept 1: La Transformación
**Hypothesis:** Cold students don't believe AI can replace their highlighter-and-rewrite ritual until they see raw apuntes turn into mind maps, flashcards and a study plan in a single on-screen beat — demo-driven proof shortcuts the skepticism barrier.
**Primary Message:** Sube tus apuntes. En segundos tienes resúmenes, mapas mentales, flashcards y un plan de estudio personalizado.
**Tone:** formal 7/10, bold 8/10, innovative 9/10, motivador, directo
**Copy Framework:** PAS — Problem (horas resumiendo a mano), Agitate (y al final no te acuerdas de nada), Solve (CognitoStudy lo hace en segundos)
**Visual Direction A (Photography — Portrait mode):** Over-shoulder portrait of a student at a dark desk surrounded by handwritten notes and a laptop glowing with `#6C63FF` UI light on their face. Single soft key light, nocturno-digital mood, the glow of the screen is the hero — not the face. Negative space top-right reserved for copy.
**Visual Direction B (Illustration — UI/Web mode):** Split-screen abstract composition. Left: chaotic stack of paper apuntes silhouettes against `#0F0F1A`. Right: clean abstract UI silhouette emitting `#6C63FF` glow with implied mind-map nodes and flashcard cards floating — no readable text. A diagonal `#00C9A7` light ray bridges left to right symbolising transformation.
**Target Platforms:** Meta Reels + TikTok (motion-native demo cuts well to 9:16); Meta Feed for static stills of the "after" state
**CTA:** Pruébalo gratis
**Addresses:** General brand awareness — establishes the core product mechanic for a cold audience

---

### Concept 2: Para Opositores
**Hypothesis:** Oposiciones students are a high-intent, high-LTV segment with a uniquely painful problem (temarios of 40-200+ topics over 1-2 years) — speaking directly to them in their own language unlocks conversion rates generic student creative cannot.
**Primary Message:** Tu temario completo, transformado en plan de estudio diario. La IA que sí entiende a los opositores.
**Tone:** formal 8/10, bold 9/10, serious 7/10 — slightly more institutional than Concept 1, less playful, respect the stakes
**Copy Framework:** PAS — Problem (temario de 300 temas), Agitate (sin método estás perdido), Solve (CognitoStudy estructura tu oposición desde el día 1)
**Visual Direction A (Photography — Portrait mode):** Tight close-up of hands turning a thick oposiciones BOE-style binder under a single warm desk lamp, `#0F0F1A` shadow background, the laptop screen out of focus with a faint `#3B82F6` UI glow in the bokeh. Quiet authority, exam-night atmosphere, lower 30% kept minimal for copy overlay.
**Visual Direction B (Illustration — UI/Web mode):** Vertical abstract "tower of temas" — 200+ stacked rectangular blocks in muted `#0F0F1A` greys ascending out of frame, with a luminous `#6C63FF` vertical line slicing through them and resolving at the bottom into a clean structured calendar/plan silhouette. Visual metaphor: chaos → structure. Generous negative space all sides.
**Target Platforms:** Meta Feed (1:1 and 4:5 best for serious-tone static creative); Meta Reels for testimonial-style talking-head variants. Lower priority on TikTok (younger skew).
**CTA:** Empieza tu plan gratis
**Addresses:** High-intent niche segment capture — opositores convert at higher rates and have longer retention than general students

---

### Concept 3: vs Todos
**Hypothesis:** Students already comparing Quizlet, NotebookLM and Notion are bottom-of-funnel and warm — a challenger frame that names the alternatives' weaknesses (fragmentation, manual work, no plan) and positions CognitoStudy as the unified system intercepts that consideration.
**Primary Message:** Quizlet hace flashcards. NotebookLM resume. Notion organiza. CognitoStudy hace todo — y te da el plan.
**Tone:** bold 9/10, playful 6/10, confident challenger — slightly more cheeky than the other two concepts, but still tecnológico-cercano not aggressive
**Copy Framework:** PAS — Problem (usas 4 apps para estudiar), Agitate (y ninguna te dice qué estudiar hoy), Solve (CognitoStudy es el sistema completo)
**Visual Direction A (Photography — Portrait mode):** Phone-in-hand POV shot, dark nocturno-digital setting, the phone screen glowing `#6C63FF` while three other phones lie face-down and dimmed on the desk surface. Suggests one app replacing many without naming competitors visually. Right 40% minimal for copy.
**Visual Direction B (Illustration — UI/Web mode):** 2x2 grid composition on `#0F0F1A`. Three quadrants show fragmented, disconnected abstract UI silhouettes (a lone flashcard shape, a lone document shape, a lone checklist shape) in muted greys. The fourth quadrant — bottom right — bursts open with a unified `#6C63FF` glowing ecosystem of all three elements connected by `#00C9A7` lines. Stark contrast between fragmentation and integration.
**Target Platforms:** Meta Reels + TikTok (comparison hooks perform well in scroll-stopping 9:16 format); Meta Feed for static challenger statements
**CTA:** Cambia de método gratis
**Addresses:** Competitor-aware bottom-funnel intercept — captures users actively evaluating alternatives
